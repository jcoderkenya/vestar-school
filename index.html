About Vestar School

A Dynamic Center of Excellence in Chuka.

Situated in the calm, scenic Kibumbu area along the Chuka–Kiang’ondu Forest Road, Vestar School provides an inspiring environment free from urban noise, allowing young minds to focus, discover their gifts, and thrive academically.

Our Vision

“To be a dynamic center of excellence in teaching, learning, and service to the community.”

Our Mission

“To provide quality education as well as inculcate practical skills for community development.”

Curriculum Structure

Grade 1 to Grade 6

  • ✓ Talent development (football, drama, music)

Safe & Dependable

School Bus Fleet & Pick-Up Routes.

We operate a fleet of well-maintained school buses with qualified drivers and caring bus assistants to ensure your child is picked up and dropped off safely every morning and evening.

Route 1: Chuka Town CBD & Environs Daily Doorstep
Route 2: Ndagani & Chuka University Axis Daily Doorstep
Route 3: Kibumbu & Kiang’ondu Forest Road Daily Doorstep
Route 4: Magutuni & Extended Environs By Arrangement

Child Safety & Transport Standards

  • ✓ Dedicated Bus Minders: Every bus has an attendant to assist young learners getting on and off safely.
  • ✓ Speed Governor & Seat Belts: Strictly compliant with Ministry of Transport school safety regulations.
  • ✓ Parent SMS Alerts: Timely notifications regarding departure and arrival schedules.
